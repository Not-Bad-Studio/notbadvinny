Service

Client

el Tab

For el Tab, I was integral in crafting the brand's identity, designing both the initial launch website and the subsequent website to accommodate its growing popularity. Additionally, I led the creation of the UX/UI for their application, ensuring a seamless and engaging user experience.

My role extended beyond design to include the technical build of the platforms, establishing a coherent and appealing digital presence that effectively communicates el Tab's unique value proposition in the nightlife and hospitality industry.
